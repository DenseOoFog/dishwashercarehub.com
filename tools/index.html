What these tools can and cannot do

  • They organize observable clues and transparent calculations; they do not remotely test the appliance.
  • Model-specific owner documentation remains the authority for parts, cycles, filter access, and installation.
  • Stop using the appliance for smoke, scorching, repeated breaker trips, active leaking near electricity, or a burning smell.
  • Do not remove panels or work on energized electrical, gas, refrigerant, or concealed plumbing components based on a tool result.
