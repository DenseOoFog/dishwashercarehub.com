Drain-path decision tool

Why Is Water Standing in My Dishwasher?

Use clues you can see and hear—without opening service panels—to find the most sensible next check.

This tool separates normal sump water from filter trouble, sink-side backup, disposal installation mistakes, backflow, and faults that need service.

What counts as standing water?

A small amount below the filter surface can be normal on many models because seals and pumps may retain water. A visible pool across the flat tub floor, dirty water covering the filter, or water that returns after draining deserves attention.

Compare the level with your owner’s manual. Do not reach into cloudy water until power is disconnected; broken glass can hide below the surface.

The drain path this tool follows

  1. Filter and filter well inside the tub.
  2. Drain hose route under the sink.
  3. Air gap or high-loop backflow protection.
  4. Garbage disposal inlet or sink drain connection.
  5. Drain pump, wiring, and controls—which belong with qualified service.

Use the full standing-water sequence

After the tool narrows the timing and sink-side clues, continue with Dishwasher Standing Water After Cycle. If the tub is too full to inspect safely, begin with How to Drain a Dishwasher with Standing Water.
