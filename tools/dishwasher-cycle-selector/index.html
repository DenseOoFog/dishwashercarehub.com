What the common cycle names usually mean

Normal

Everyday, normally soiled mixed tableware. This is the safest general starting point when no special condition dominates.

Auto or Sensor

Measures soil-related conditions and adjusts some combination of time, water, or energy. Useful for a genuinely mixed load.

Heavy or Pots & Pans

Longer or more vigorous washing for dishwasher-safe cookware and dried-on, baked-on, or greasy soil.

Quick or 1-Hour

Fast turnaround for lightly soiled essentials. It may use more water, heat, or energy to save time.

Delicate, China, or Crystal

Gentler action or lower heat for items explicitly approved for machine washing. Item-care instructions still control.

Rinse Only

A short rinse to reduce dried-on food or odor before a later full cycle. It does not replace washing.

Cycle labels are not standardized across every brand. Some buttons are options rather than complete cycles, and the same name may include different temperatures or drying behavior. Read the cycle table for your exact model before relying on heat-sensitive, delicate, or sanitizing settings.

Set up the load before choosing

Clear the filter, make sure each spray arm can turn, avoid nesting bowls, keep the detergent dispenser unobstructed, and scrape large debris. A stronger program cannot fully compensate for blocked water paths or an overloaded rack.
