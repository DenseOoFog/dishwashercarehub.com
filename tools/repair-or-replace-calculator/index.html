Dishwasher Repair or Replace Calculator

Compare the quote you received with replacement cost, appliance age, and repair history. The result explains the tradeoffs instead of pretending there is one universal rule.

Your entries remain on this device. Prices are in US dollars and should include labor, parts, delivery, installation, and haul-away when applicable.

How the calculation works

The tool divides the repair quote by the installed replacement price. It then considers age, repeat repairs, major-component work, and safety or water-damage signals. It does not assume that every repair above an arbitrary percentage is wasteful.

  • Repair cost ratio: repair quote ÷ installed replacement price.
  • History adjustment: repeat faults make another repair less predictable.
  • Age adjustment: an older machine has less time to spread the repair cost over.
  • Safety adjustment: leaks, scorching, and electrical symptoms require qualified inspection, regardless of the calculator result.

Before accepting either estimate

  • Ask whether the diagnostic fee is included in the repair quote.
  • Confirm the parts-and-labor warranty and whether a return visit is covered.
  • For replacement, include installation parts, permit or electrical work, delivery, and haul-away.
  • Check whether a leak damaged flooring or cabinets; those costs are separate from the appliance decision.
  • Get a second diagnosis when a major component is blamed without clear testing.
