Dishwasher Detergent Amount Guide

Use your water hardness, detergent format, load soil, and current symptom to find a cautious starting point—and the next variable to check before adding more detergent.

This tool runs entirely in your browser. It does not save your answers and does not replace the dose table in your dishwasher manual or detergent label.

How the starting guide is calculated

For loose powder, liquid, or gel, the general cup-fill bands are based on GE Appliances guidance: about one-third full for 0–3 gpg, two-thirds for more than 3–8 gpg, full for more than 8–12 gpg, and a manual check for the pre-wash cup above 12 gpg. This is a broad starting framework, not a universal dose.

Dishwasher dispenser sizes, detergent concentration, cycle design, and local water chemistry vary. That is why this tool describes the dispenser fraction and an adjustment sequence instead of inventing a gram amount.

Before changing detergent

  • Confirm the hardness unit. Ten ppm is soft; 10 gpg is hard.
  • Use only automatic dishwasher detergent. Hand dish soap can create excessive suds and overflow.
  • Keep loose detergent sealed, cool, and dry. Damp or old detergent can clump and lose performance.
  • Check filter cleanliness, spray-arm movement, loading, cycle choice, and dispenser clearance before assuming the dose is too low.
  • Change one setting per load and note the result; several simultaneous changes hide the real cause.

Common questions

Dishwasher Detergent Dose Guide FAQ

  • Does this tool calculate an exact detergent dose? No. It identifies a cautious starting direction from water hardness, soil, detergent format, and symptoms. The detergent label and dishwasher manual control the permitted product, dispenser position, and dose range for a particular load and model.
  • What if I do not know my water hardness? Use the local utility report or a suitable test strip before making large dose changes. Guessing can send troubleshooting in the wrong direction because mineral film, excess detergent, poor rinsing, and permanent glass etching can look similar.
  • Should I cut or unwrap a detergent pod? Do not alter a pod unless its product instructions explicitly say to do so. Store it dry, place it in the specified dispenser, keep the dispenser path clear, and use a compatible cycle according to both product and appliance directions.
