Why timing matters more than a sound name

Normal operation can include water swishing during wash, a short hum during drain, clicks as valves or controls change state, and a fan after the cycle on some models. A rhythmic knock during wash often begins with loading or spray-arm clearance. The same hum becomes more concerning when water remains in the tub.

The tool does not listen to audio or identify a failed part. Stop for active leaking, smoke, scorching, a burning smell, or repeated breaker trips. Disconnect power before checking racks, filters, or spray-arm clearance, and never remove service panels based on a tool result.
