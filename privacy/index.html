Privacy Policy

This policy explains information handling, hosting logs, advertising technology, privacy choices, and contact options for Dishwasher Care Lab.

Information you provide

Dishwasher Care Lab does not require an account. If you contact the site by email, the message, email address, appliance details, and any other information you choose to send are used to respond, investigate a correction, and manage related follow-up. Messages are retained only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes, recordkeeping, security, or legal obligations. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.

Hosting and technical logs

The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, requested URL, referrer, approximate request location, response status, and timing. This information supports delivery, abuse prevention, security, reliability, and performance. Dishwasher Care Lab does not operate a user-account database and does not sell personal information.

Google advertising and cookies

The site contains Google AdSense publisher code, although ad serving may be unavailable while Google reviews or limits the site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to serve and measure ads when advertising is active. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on a user’s visit to this site and other sites on the Internet.

Other third-party advertising vendors or networks may also use cookies when authorized through the publisher’s Google advertising configuration. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services and read Google’s advertising technology explanation.

Advertising choices

Users can visit Google Ads Settings to control or opt out of personalized advertising from Google. Users can also visit AboutAds opt-out choices for participating third-party vendors.

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, personalized advertising requires the disclosures and choices supplied through a Google-certified consent management platform where applicable. The publisher must keep that account-level consent configuration active before serving personalized ads in those regions. This policy does not treat a basic site banner as a substitute for a certified CMP.

US state privacy choices

Google Privacy & messaging can display a “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” choice to visitors in supported US states. When that account-level message is active and a visitor opts out, Google uses the applicable privacy signal, including the Global Privacy Platform where supported, to apply the selected advertising restriction. A sentence in this policy cannot replace that interactive choice.

Visitors may also email pqiswin1@gmail.com with a privacy request. Include the state or country of residence and enough information to understand the request, but do not send identity documents or sensitive information unless specifically required through a secure process.

Children’s privacy

This site is intended for a general audience seeking appliance-care information and is not directed to children under 13. Dishwasher Care Lab does not knowingly request personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child sent personal information can use the contact address below to request review and deletion.

External links

Guides link to manufacturer support pages, government resources, and other external sites. Those sites operate under their own privacy policies and controls. Following an external link sends a normal web request to that provider.

Policy updates and contact

This page will be updated when the site changes its advertising, analytics, storage, or contact practices. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to pqiswin1@gmail.com. Include the relevant page URL and enough detail to understand the request.
